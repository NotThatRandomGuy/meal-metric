# App Idea Brainstorming

## New App Ideas - List

### 1. MealMetric
A fast-food nutritional analysis application that retrieves nutritional information for restaurant menu items and processes it into plain-English explanations. Users can evaluate the nutritional quality of their meals, understand potential effects on satiety and energy levels, calculate calories per cent spent, and receive recommendations for balancing their nutritional intake throughout the remainder of the day.

### 2. GameCompare
A video game price comparison application that allows users to search for products and compare prices between Loaded and Instant Gaming. The application also displays publicly available game descriptions, ratings, system requirements, and platform information.

### 3. CampusCompass
A campus navigation application that helps college students locate classrooms, parking lots, dining locations, and other campus facilities. Users can also manage their class schedules and receive location-based reminders.

### 4. SubscriptionWatch
A subscription management application that allows users to track recurring subscriptions, free trials, renewal dates, and monthly and annual subscription expenses. Users receive notifications before upcoming payments.

### 5. PantryPal
A household food inventory application that allows users to track available ingredients, receive expiration reminders, discover recipes based on existing ingredients, and generate shopping lists.

### 6. FuelFinder
A fuel price comparison application that evaluates nearby gas stations based on fuel prices, distance, and estimated travel expenses to determine whether traveling farther for cheaper fuel would result in meaningful savings.

## Top Three App Ideas - Evaluate and Categorize

### 1. MealMetric

- **Mobile:** The application could utilize location services to identify nearby restaurants, local notifications for dietary reminders, and the camera for receipt scanning.
- **Story:** Fast-food nutritional information is often presented as raw numerical data. This application would make that information more accessible by providing understandable interpretations and practical recommendations.
- **Market:** The application targets regular fast-food consumers, college students, budget-conscious individuals, and individuals interested in monitoring their nutritional intake.
- **Habit:** Users could interact with the application whenever they purchase fast food, plan meals, or review their daily nutritional intake.
- **Scope:** The minimum viable product would include restaurant menu retrieval, meal construction, nutritional analysis, and calorie-to-price calculations. Additional features could be implemented incrementally.
- **API Availability:** USDA FoodData Central provides a free nutritional API. Restaurant-specific nutritional databases will also be evaluated for coverage and accessibility.

### 2. GameCompare

- **Mobile:** The application could utilize push notifications for price reductions and mobile sharing capabilities for game deals.
- **Story:** Users could compare video game prices across multiple storefronts without manually searching each website.
- **Market:** The application targets video game consumers, particularly individuals interested in purchasing discounted digital games.
- **Habit:** Users would primarily interact with the application when considering a purchase or monitoring discounts.
- **Scope:** The basic interface and comparison functionality would be manageable. However, reliably retrieving current pricing information from multiple storefronts could introduce additional technical challenges.
- **API Availability:** Public game information could potentially be retrieved through APIs such as RAWG. Free and reliable access to pricing information from both storefronts would require further investigation.

### 3. PantryPal

- **Mobile:** The application could utilize the camera for barcode scanning and local notifications for expiration reminders.
- **Story:** The application would help users reduce household food waste and discover meals that can be prepared using ingredients they already own.
- **Market:** The application targets households, college students, and individuals interested in reducing grocery expenses.
- **Habit:** Users could interact with the application when purchasing groceries, preparing meals, or managing their household food inventory.
- **Scope:** A basic inventory management system would be relatively manageable, with recipe recommendations and barcode scanning serving as potential extensions.
- **API Availability:** Open Food Facts provides a publicly accessible food product database. Recipe APIs could also be evaluated for integration.

## Final App Idea

**Selected Application: MealMetric**

After evaluating the three shortlisted application ideas, MealMetric was selected as the final capstone project.

The application addresses a practical problem by helping individuals understand the nutritional and economic implications of their fast-food purchases. Rather than exclusively displaying numerical nutritional information, MealMetric will process this information into understandable explanations and actionable recommendations.

The application also provides a sufficiently flexible scope for individual development. Its core functionality can be implemented through an external nutritional database and a custom processing algorithm, while additional features can be introduced throughout the development process.

The combination of nutritional interpretation, economic analysis, and daily dietary recommendations makes MealMetric a distinct and practical application.
